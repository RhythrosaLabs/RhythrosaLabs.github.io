The official portfolio and website for **Daniel Sheils** — a multi-disciplinary creative working across sound design, music production, AI software, game development, visual arts, and interactive media since 2008.

## 🧑‍🎨 About

**Daniel Sheils** is a Missoula, MT-based creator and founder of Noodlebake Records. As **Scatterboxx**, he has written and produced nearly 30 albums. As a sound designer, he has built immersive AR/VR/XR soundscapes for **Red Bull, Microsoft, Intel, Amazon, Motorola, Lenovo, San Diego Padres, The Glenlivet**, and the **Alzheimer's Association**. He is an official **Streamlit Creator**, recognized for building autonomous AI tools for creative industries.
